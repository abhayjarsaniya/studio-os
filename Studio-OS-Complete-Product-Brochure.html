Control whether each assigned crew member sees the shoot on their personal calendar. Toggle Show/Hide visibility with a single click to keep schedules uncluttered.

CRITICAL SAFETY SHIELD

Intelligent Scheduling Conflict Engine

Studio OS continuously evaluates all event dates, call times, and team allocations in the background.

STEP 1 Background Overlap Scan

When assigning crew to an event or sub-event, the engine scans all other bookings for that studio on the same date.

STEP 2 Subtle Conflict Icon

If a member is already booked, Studio OS displays a subtle amber/red warning icon directly beside their name.

STEP 3 Detailed Conflict Popover

Tapping the icon reveals the conflicting booking ID, event title, venue, and overlapping hours.

STEP 4 Owner Operational Discretion

Warnings are informative and non-blocking—allowing owners to make emergency overrides when timing permits.

MODULE BREAKDOWN

Deliverables & 7-Stage Post-Production Pipeline

Post-production bottlenecks cause immense friction between studios and wedding couples. Studio OS implements a standardized 7-stage deliverables tracking engine with target offset days and completion checklists.

01
RAW Photo & Video Backup Verified Target: Day +1 (Within 24 Hours)

Footage dumped from camera memory cards onto local studio NAS and secondary cloud storage. Checksum verified before cards are formatted.

02
Photo Culling & Selection Link Dispatched Target: Day +3

Duplicates and blinks removed. Curated photo selection gallery generated and sent to the couple via WhatsApp/Email link.

03
Customer Photo Selection Received Target: Day +10

Couple selects their favorite 300–400 photographs for album layout and album cover portraits.

04
Master Photo Retouching & Color Grading Target: Day +20

Skin retouching, light corrections, and high-end artistic color grading completed on all selected wedding photographs.

05
Cinematic Teaser & Instagram Reels Delivered Target: Day +7

60-second vertical 9:16 wedding teaser edited with licensed audio for immediate social media sharing by the couple.

06
Full Feature Wedding Cinema Film Delivered Target: Day +30

30–45 minute documentary edit with multi-camera audio sync, vows, speeches, and color-graded cinematic drone footage.

07
Final Quality Control & Client Handover Target: Day +50

Master engraved crystal pendrive loaded with 4K files, printed photobooks verified, balance collected, and booking closed.
